 Problem statement :  
   Access to clean and safe water is a critical necessity for households, agriculture, and industries. 
However, water contamination due to pollutants, chemical imbalances, and microbial presence 
poses a significant threat to health and productivity. Traditional water quality testing methods are 
often time-consuming, expensive, and require manual intervention, making real-time monitoring 
challenging.


Proposed solution :           
      Aqua Sentinel is a smart water quality monitoring system that continuously measures pH, turbidity, 
temperature, and dissolved oxygen using advanced sensors. The device processes data in real-time 
and sends alerts via Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or GSM if water quality deviates from safe levels. A user
friendly mobile/web dashboard provides live data and insights. Applicable in households, agriculture, 
industries, and aquaculture, this solution ensures safe, efficient, and sustainable water usage through 
real-time monitoring and alerts.

Project Outcome:
 Aqua Sentinel will deliver a real-time water quality monitoring system that ensures safe and 
sustainable water usage. It will provide accurate parameter tracking, instant alerts, and a user
friendly dashboard for data insights. The system will be cost-effective, scalable, and applicable in 
households, agriculture, industries, and aquaculture, enhancing water safety and efficiency.
